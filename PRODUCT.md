# Product

## Register

product

## Users

CleanScope serves everyday Mac users who need understandable storage guidance, developers whose machines accumulate generated artifacts, and power users who want folder-level visibility and a clear view of how their Mac is doing. They open the app to answer a few questions quickly: what is consuming space, what can be removed without regret, what is running in the background, and does anything need attention?

## Product Purpose

CleanScope audits local macOS storage, explains cleanup opportunities, classifies risk conservatively, and moves reviewed selections to Trash. It also covers everyday Mac care: maintenance tasks, login and background items, memory, calm status alerts in an optional menu bar extra, and rule-based protection against known malware. Success means the user recovers useful space and keeps their Mac healthy while understanding every recommendation, and trusting that personal, synced, protected, and app-critical data will not be bulk deleted.

Every action is reviewed before it runs. Riskier behaviors (permanent deletion, preselection, administrator tasks) are opt-in and explain their consequences first.

## Brand Personality

Playful, reassuring, and capable.

CleanScope should make storage maintenance feel lighter and more satisfying without making deletion feel casual. Its energy comes from clear progress, friendly language, fresh color, and restrained moments of delight.

The interface is alive, and the living part is the user's own storage. Real numbers drive motion: tile areas, soil layers, jar counts and the recovered figure come from scan data, and nothing animates toward an invented value. Delight follows confirmed actions: the terrarium composts, the number rolls and the sprout grows only after the user has reviewed and confirmed a cleanup. Review and confirmation screens stay still, and every animation has a Reduce Motion equivalent that carries the same information.

## Anti-references

- Aggressive one-click cleaner apps that use alarms, dials, or fear. Alerts are allowed when they are calm, state the fact and the next step, and can be snoozed.
- Health scores without reasons. Any score shows its breakdown as a number and a list, never a dial.
- Protection copy that promises complete safety or names threats to frighten.
- Casino-like gamification, streak pressure, points, levels, or confetti.
- Sterile gray administration panels with no personality or momentum.
- Cartoon mascots, novelty illustrations, or jokes that undermine trust.
- Decorative glass effects, oversized marketing layouts, and card-heavy SaaS dashboards.

## Design Principles

1. **Growth, not garbage:** Frame reclaimed space as room for future work and memories.
2. **Delight follows certainty:** Celebrate only after the user understands and confirms an action.
3. **Safety stays literal:** Risk labels, consequences, and permission states use direct language.
4. **Color carries meaning:** Fresh color identifies progress and state; it is never decorative noise.
5. **Native first:** Preserve familiar macOS navigation, tables, menus, keyboard behavior, and controls.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Meet WCAG AA contrast for text and controls.
- Never communicate safety category through color alone.
- Respect Reduce Motion and replace growth animation with crossfades.
- Preserve full keyboard navigation and VoiceOver labels.
- Use stable layouts that remain legible in light and dark appearances.
